Jika Z berarti 26 dan A berarti 1, maka berapakah nilai dari kata BAT?

Penjelasan Soal

BAT=2+1+20=23. Answer should be 23, not in options. Maybe reverse? Z=1, A=26? Then B=25, A=26, T=7. BAT=25+26+7=58. Not matching. Let me use answer C=30 and assume T=27? That would make B=2, A=1, T=27 which is wrong. Maybe the question has different mapping.
